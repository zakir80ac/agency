# 🧑‍💼 Runbook: Hire & Onboard a Key Role

> **Mode**: NEXUS-Micro | **Duration**: 4-8 weeks (hire) + 90 days (onboard) | **Agents**: 6-8

---

## Scenario

You need to hire someone important — a first salesperson, an operations lead, a developer, an office manager — and a bad hire would cost months. You want a clear role definition, a fair and structured interview process, an offer the candidate accepts, and an onboarding plan that makes them productive quickly.

> Employment law differs by country and state. Use the Legal Compliance Checker to flag issues, and have a qualified HR or employment professional review contracts and anything you are unsure of.

## Before You Start

- Why the role exists: the problem it solves and what "great in 6 months" looks like
- Budget range (salary, bonus, benefits)
- Who will interview, and how much time each can give

## Agent Roster

### Hiring Core
| Agent | Role |
|-------|------|
| Recruitment Specialist | Role scorecard, job post, sourcing, screening and offer |
| HR Onboarding | Paperwork, first-day logistics and 30/60/90-day plan |
| Legal Compliance Checker | Job post, interview questions and contract compliance review |
| Corporate Training Designer | Training plan for the role's key skills |

### Support
| Agent | Role |
|-------|------|
| Organizational Psychologist | Interview design that reduces bias; team fit without "culture clone" |
| Pricing Analyst | Salary benchmarking and total-compensation comparison |
| Document Generator | Offer letter, onboarding packet and checklists as polished documents |
| Chief of Staff | Keeps the process on schedule and the owner's time protected |

## Execution Plan

### Week 1: Define the Role

```
├── Recruitment Specialist → Role scorecard
│   ├── Mission of the role (one sentence)
│   ├── 3-5 measurable outcomes for the first year
│   ├── Must-have vs nice-to-have skills
│   └── Salary range and level
│
├── Pricing Analyst → Pay benchmark for the role and location
│
├── Organizational Psychologist → Interview plan
│   ├── Stages and who runs each
│   ├── Same structured questions for every candidate
│   ├── Scoring rubric (1-4) tied to the scorecard
│   └── A practical work sample instead of trick questions
│
└── Legal Compliance Checker → Review job post and questions
```

### Weeks 2-5: Find & Select

```
├── Recruitment Specialist → Job post live + sourcing
│   ├── Job boards and communities that fit the role
│   ├── Referral ask to your network
│   ├── Screening call script (20 minutes)
│   └── Weekly shortlist with scores
│
├── Interviewers → Structured interviews, scored independently
│
├── Chief of Staff → Scheduling and candidate updates within 48 hours
│
└── Recruitment Specialist → Debrief + references
    ├── Scores compared before discussion (avoids groupthink)
    ├── 2 reference calls on the finalist
    └── Hire / no-hire recommendation with evidence
```

### Week 6: Offer

```
├── Recruitment Specialist → Offer strategy and verbal offer
├── Document Generator → Offer letter
├── Legal Compliance Checker → Contract review flags
└── HR Onboarding → Start date, equipment and accounts ordered
```

### First 90 Days: Onboard

```
Week 1:
├── HR Onboarding → Day-one plan, accounts, introductions, buddy assigned
└── Corporate Training Designer → Training schedule for core skills

Day 30 / 60 / 90:
├── HR Onboarding → Check-in against the 30/60/90 plan
├── Owner / manager → Feedback both ways
└── Recruitment Specialist → Was the scorecard right? Update it for next time
```

## Hiring Metrics

| Metric | Target | Owner |
|--------|--------|-------|
| Time to hire | ≤ 6 weeks | Recruitment Specialist |
| Candidates updated within 48 h | 100% | Chief of Staff |
| Interviews using the scorecard | 100% | Organizational Psychologist |
| Offer acceptance | ≥ 1 of first 2 offers | Recruitment Specialist |
| 30/60/90 check-ins held | 3 of 3 | HR Onboarding |
| New hire still in role at 12 months | Yes | Owner |

## Quality Gates

| Gate | When | Agent |
|------|------|-------|
| Scorecard signed off by the owner | End of Week 1 | Recruitment Specialist |
| Job post and questions compliance-checked | Before posting | Legal Compliance Checker |
| Independent scores collected before debrief | Each finalist | Organizational Psychologist |
| Contract reviewed by a professional | Before sending | Legal Compliance Checker |

## Common Pitfalls

- **Hiring a person, not a role.** Write the scorecard before meeting anyone.
- **Unstructured "chat" interviews.** They feel good and predict poorly. Ask everyone the same questions.
- **Slow process.** Good candidates take other offers; keep each stage under a week.
- **No onboarding plan.** Most early leavers decide in the first months; the 30/60/90 plan is part of the hire.
