# 💼 Runbook: Lead-to-Close Sales Sprint

> **Mode**: NEXUS-Sprint | **Duration**: 4-6 weeks (then repeat monthly) | **Agents**: 8-11

---

## Scenario

You sell a product or service and the pipeline is thin, unpredictable, or full of deals that never close. You want a repeatable motion: a clear offer, a steady flow of qualified conversations, proposals that win, and a forecast you can trust. This runbook works for a founder selling alone as well as a small sales team.

## Before You Start

Have these ready (rough is fine — the agents will sharpen them):

- A one-paragraph description of what you sell and what it costs
- Your 3-5 best current or past customers, and why they bought
- Where deals live today (CRM, spreadsheet, inbox)
- Last quarter's numbers if you have them: leads, meetings, proposals, wins

## Agent Roster

### Pipeline Core
| Agent | Role |
|-------|------|
| Offer & Lead Gen Strategist | Sharpens the offer and the ideal customer profile; designs lead magnets |
| Outbound Strategist | Builds target lists and multi-touch outreach sequences |
| Discovery Coach | Prepares discovery calls and qualification questions |
| Deal Strategist | Plans how to win each serious opportunity |
| Proposal Strategist | Writes proposals and pricing options that close |
| Pipeline Analyst | Tracks stage conversion, deal velocity and forecast accuracy |

### Support
| Agent | Role |
|-------|------|
| Sales Coach | Weekly review of calls and deals; skill development |
| Pricing Analyst | Price points, packaging and discount guardrails |
| Sales Data Extraction Agent | Pulls deal and activity data into one clean dataset |
| Account Strategist | Expansion and renewal plays once deals close |
| Executive Summary Generator | One-page weekly sales summary for the owner |

## Execution Plan

### Week 1: Offer & Targets

```
Day 1-2: Offer Clarity
├── Offer & Lead Gen Strategist → Ideal customer profile + offer
│   ├── Who buys, who doesn't, and why
│   ├── The problem in the customer's words
│   ├── Offer statement and proof points
│   └── One lead magnet or entry offer
│
├── Pricing Analyst → Packaging review
│   ├── Good / better / best options
│   ├── Minimum acceptable price and discount rules
│   └── What to say when asked "can you do it cheaper?"
│
└── Pipeline Analyst → Baseline
    ├── Current stage definitions (write them down)
    ├── Conversion rate per stage
    └── Average deal size and sales cycle length

Day 3-5: Targets & Sequences
├── Outbound Strategist → Target list (100-300 accounts) + sequences
│   ├── Trigger events worth reaching out on
│   ├── 3 messaging angles to test
│   └── 6-8 touch sequence across email, phone and LinkedIn
│
└── Discovery Coach → Discovery call kit
    ├── 8-10 open questions in a logical order
    ├── Qualification criteria (budget, authority, need, timing)
    └── "Disqualify early" signals
```

### Weeks 2-4: Run the Motion

```
Daily:
├── Outbound Strategist → Sequences running; replies routed same day
└── Discovery Coach → Prep notes before each discovery call

Per qualified opportunity:
├── Deal Strategist → Win plan
│   ├── Decision makers and their priorities
│   ├── Competition and the "do nothing" option
│   ├── Risks and how to close them
│   └── Mutual action plan with dates
│
└── Proposal Strategist → Proposal
    ├── Customer problem restated in their words
    ├── Outcome and measurable value
    ├── 2-3 priced options
    └── Clear next step and expiry date

Weekly:
├── Sales Coach → Review 2-3 calls and the top 5 deals
├── Sales Data Extraction Agent → Refresh the pipeline dataset
├── Pipeline Analyst → Forecast: commit / best case / pipeline
└── Executive Summary Generator → One-page update for the owner
```

### Weeks 5-6: Tune & Make It Repeatable

```
├── Pipeline Analyst → What converted, what stalled, where deals died
├── Outbound Strategist → Kill the weakest messaging angle, double the best
├── Proposal Strategist → Turn the winning proposal into a template
├── Account Strategist → Onboarding and expansion plan for new customers
└── Sales Coach → Written playbook: the steps that actually worked
```

## Sales Metrics

| Metric | Target | Owner |
|--------|--------|-------|
| Reply rate on outreach | > 5% (cold), > 15% (warm) | Outbound Strategist |
| Meetings booked per week | [Set from baseline + 25%] | Outbound Strategist |
| Discovery → proposal rate | > 40% | Discovery Coach |
| Proposal → win rate | > 30% | Proposal Strategist |
| Average discount given | Within the agreed guardrail | Pricing Analyst |
| Forecast accuracy (commit) | Within ±15% of actual | Pipeline Analyst |
| Sales cycle length | Trending down month over month | Deal Strategist |

Targets are starting points; replace them with your own baseline after Week 1.

## Quality Gates

| Gate | When | Agent |
|------|------|-------|
| Offer and ICP agreed by the owner | End of Week 1 | Offer & Lead Gen Strategist |
| Every deal past discovery has a win plan | Before proposal | Deal Strategist |
| Proposal reviewed against pricing rules | Before sending | Pricing Analyst |
| Forecast reviewed with evidence, not hope | Weekly | Pipeline Analyst |

## Common Pitfalls

- **Pitching before qualifying.** Discovery first; a proposal for an unqualified buyer wastes a week.
- **One contact per deal.** If your only contact goes quiet, the deal dies. Aim for two or more people involved.
- **Discounting to close.** Trade price for something (longer term, faster payment, case study), never for nothing.
- **Pipeline you hope for.** A deal without a dated next step is not in the forecast.
